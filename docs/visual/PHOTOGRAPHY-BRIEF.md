# Photography brief and commissioned-coverage gap list

## Brief

Commission Harlem-based documentary photographers for current place, neighborhood, and event coverage. The work should read as editorial documentation, not advertising. Real storefronts, real interiors where permitted, real public vantage points, and people only with consent.

Minimum per canonical place:

- one landscape hero image suitable for a 16:9 editorial lead
- at least three gallery images with distinct information: establishing view, identifying detail, interior/activity/context where permitted
- one square-safe or 4:3 crop source for cards
- documented alt text, capture date, creator credit, contract/license identifier, source file, and consent notes where people appear
- enough resolution for responsive web and native use; do not ship social-media reposts or venue images without written rights

Rights deliverable: a written license granting Harlem Might use in the app, product site, and product communications, with any required attribution string recorded exactly. Record whether derivatives/crops and share-alike obligations apply before ingest.
