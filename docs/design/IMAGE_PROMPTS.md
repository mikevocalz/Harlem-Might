# Company page images

Save each image to `apps/web/public/images/company/<file>` (WebP, JPG or PNG).
It appears as soon as the file exists. Images render without captions.

Keep scenes generic: no recreation of a specific real landmark or storefront,
no recognisable people, no readable signs. Pages about a specific real place
use real photographs.

## Why the first round looked fake

"Hyper-realistic", "cinematic", "golden hour" and "rich shadows" push the
model toward a glossy, too-perfect look. The prompts below name real
professional gear and settings for the image quality, and real-world light,
clutter and imperfection for believability.

## Shared style (paste in front of every prompt)

> A real photograph by a working New York documentary photographer, shot on a Leica SL3 with a Summilux-SL 35mm f/1.4 lens at f/2.8, ISO 400, natural available light only, RAW file processed lightly in Lightroom with a neutral profile. Exceptional sharpness and micro-detail where in focus, true lens rendering with natural falloff and very slight vignetting, fine organic grain in the shadows, accurate white balance, realistic dynamic range (some highlights allowed to clip softly, shadows allowed to go dark). Real, unstaged moment with genuine urban texture and everyday clutter: chipped paint, grime on stone, scuffed steps, trash bags at the curb, parked cars, window AC units, fire escapes, scaffolding. Colors natural and restrained, like a New York Times photo essay. No text, no readable signs, no logos, no watermark, no recognisable people. Not CGI, not illustrated, not cinematic color grading, not HDR, no glow, no oversaturation, no perfect symmetry, no retouched skin or surfaces.

Medium-format option for the two hero images (about-hero, press-hero): replace the camera line with *"shot on a Fujifilm GFX100 II with a GF 45mm f/2.8 lens"*.

If a result still looks too clean, add: *"Less polished: flatter light, more ordinary, keep the real-world clutter and imperfections."*

## Prompts

### about-hero.webp (16:9, 1600×900)
A block of brownstone row houses on a residential Harlem street on an overcast weekday afternoon. Stoops with black iron railings, a couple of trash cans chained to a railing, a parked delivery van partly cutting into the left edge of the frame, bare-ish street trees with a few leaves, a scaffold on one house further down. Taken from the opposite sidewalk at standing height, framing very slightly off level. One person far down the block, walking away, small and blurred.

### about-one-place.webp (4:3, 1200×900)
A phone held in someone's hand in front of a brownstone stoop, screen showing a dark map app with one yellow pin. The phone and thumb are in focus; the stoop and front door behind are slightly soft. Daylight, a bit of glare on the phone screen, a fingerprint smudge visible. Only a hand and part of a jacket sleeve in frame.

### about-history.webp (4:3, 1200×900)
An old shuttered neighbourhood movie theater on a busy Harlem avenue at dusk, its vertical blade sign and marquee unlit and blank, metal roll-down gates closed, some faded posters removed leaving empty frames, a bodega awning next door, cars and a bus passing as motion blur. Streetlights just switching on. Shot handheld at ISO 3200, natural grain, no flash.

### about-method.webp (4:3, 1200×900)
Overhead shot of a cluttered kitchen table: a pile of old black-and-white photos of city streets (curled, creased, some stained), a spiral notebook with messy handwriting too blurry to read, a folded paper subway map, a pen, a half-finished cup of coffee with a ring stain on the table. Daylight from a window on one side, one corner of the table in shadow.

### about-sources.webp (4:3, 1200×900)
Inside a public library reading area on a weekday: long wooden tables, green desk lamps, a few laptops and bags left on chairs, metal shelves with grey archive boxes and binders at the back, fluorescent ceiling lights mixed with daylight from tall windows. A couple of people reading with their backs to the camera, out of focus.

### press-hero.webp (16:9, 1600×900)
A woman sitting on a brownstone stoop looking at her phone, photographed from across the street so she is small in the frame and her face isn't clear. Tote bag beside her, takeout coffee on the step. A parked car and part of a tree in the foreground, slightly out of focus. Mid-afternoon, partly cloudy.

### legal-privacy.webp (16:9, 1600×900)
A Harlem side street at night, shot handheld at f/1.4, ISO 3200, 1/30s. Brownstones with mostly dark windows, one lit window with a curtain, sodium streetlights giving an orange cast, a parked car with a reflection on its roof, a locked bike on a railing. Visible high-ISO grain and a touch of softness from the handheld exposure.

### legal-terms.webp (16:9, 1600×900)
A man on a sidewalk seen from behind, phone in his hand at his side, looking up at the cornices of a row of brownstones. Low iron fence and small front yards with a few plants and a recycling bin. Flat grey daylight. Taken from a few meters behind him at chest height.

### legal-accessibility.webp (16:9, 1600×900)
A brownstone building where a concrete wheelchair ramp with a metal handrail runs alongside the original stone stoop. A person in a manual wheelchair is partway up the ramp, seen from behind. At the corner, a curb cut with yellow tactile paving. Cloudy morning, wet pavement, an ordinary everyday scene, nothing posed.
