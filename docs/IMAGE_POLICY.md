# Harlem Might image policy

## Public-site rule

- Do **not** use Pexels, Unsplash, random stock-photo libraries, or hotlinked search images.
- Marketing/concept imagery may be custom-generated as **hyper-realistic original imagery** and stored as a first-party asset with prompt/provenance.
- Generated imagery must never be presented as documentary proof of a real business's current storefront, menu, staff, crowd, accessibility, operating condition, or event.
- If generated imagery depicts a recognizable real place in a materially altered or speculative state, label it as an illustration/concept.

## Place and history records

For actual place detail, Then/Now, historical claims, or source-sensitive stories, use:
1. commissioned Harlem Might photography;
2. rights-cleared venue photography;
3. Wikimedia Commons with compatible licensing;
4. NYPL / Library of Congress / The Met open material where rights permit.

Always preserve creator, source, rights/license, date, and required credit.

## Generated-image quality bar

Generated public imagery should be photorealistic, naturally lit, physically plausible, culturally specific without caricature, and free of fake signage or invented factual claims. Avoid generic "AI city" compositions, impossible architecture, plastic skin, duplicated pedestrians, mangled text, and cyberpunk neon unless the scene is explicitly a speculative AR concept.

## Storage

Generated assets are first-party media. Store the final approved file plus prompt, generation metadata, review status, alt text, and usage context in the media-rights pipeline.
